How do I find my TV's MAC address?

Open your TV's network or WiFi settings menu — the MAC address (sometimes called "device address") is usually listed there. On most smart TVs: Settings → Network → Network Status → View Additional Settings.

One device per package. Each package works on a single device only — don't buy one package to share across several devices.

WiFi won't connect

Restart WiFi on your device, forget the "Konnect Free" network, then rejoin and reload this page.WiFi yangu haitafti — zima na uwashe WiFi, kisha ujiunge tena.

Voucher expired

Your voucher's data or time has run out — buy a new package or top up above.Voucher yangu imekwisha — nunua kifurushi kipya hapo juu.

Internet is slow

Speed can dip when the dish has many connected devices at once — try moving closer to the router or reconnecting.Intaneti ni polepole — jaribu kusogea karibu na router au ku-reconnect.

Login page not showing

Open any website in your browser (e.g. google.com) — the sign-in page should pop up automatically. If not, use the "Connect your TV/Device" form above.Ukurasa wa login hauonyeshi — fungua tovuti yoyote kwenye kivinjari chako.
